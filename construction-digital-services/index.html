A few things, answered

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Every business is different. Let’s talk about what yours needs.

Can you improve my existing construction website?

Yes. We can discuss updates to an existing website or a rebuild, depending on its current condition and what your business needs.

Can you manage an existing Google Ads account?

Yes. We can help with an existing account as well as a new setup. We first review the goals, current campaigns, service areas, and budget.

What do you need from me to build the website?

Your services, service areas, business contact details, and any branding or project photos you want to use. We’ll discuss the content and priorities before starting.

Does SEO depend on paying for Google Ads?

No. Your site’s search foundations are part of the website work. Google Ads campaigns are a separate way to reach potential customers; you do not need an active campaign to have a search-friendly website.
